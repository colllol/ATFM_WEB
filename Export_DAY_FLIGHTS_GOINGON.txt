HUONG DAN EXPORT/IMPORT T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON THANG 07/2026
================================================================

Muc tieu
--------

Chuyen cac dong T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON co FLIGHTDATE trong thang 07/2026
tu Oracle nguon sang T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON tren Oracle dich qua schema tam
ATFM_STAGE. Quy trinh khong xoa du lieu dang co tren schema ATFM.

T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON la bang lich dang khai thac, co the rat lon (co the hang trieu dong)
va dang duoc cac job cap nhat MOVEFINISH/ATD/ATA. Vi vay chi export pham vi
ngay can chuyen, kiem tra xung dot khoa, sau do APPEND
vao bang dich khi cac dieu kien an toan deu dat.

Phan pham vi mac dinh:

    FLIGHTDATE >= DATE '2026-07-01'
    FLIGHTDATE <  DATE '2026-08-01'

Neu can thang khac, thay dong loc ngay trong tat ca parfile va cau lenh
kiem tra truoc khi bat dau. Khong dung TO_CHAR(FLIGHTDATE) trong dieu kien
loc vi co the lam mat kha nang su dung index ngay.

Ket qua cung phai bang 0.

11.3. Kiem tra duplicate khoa tren dich:

SELECT ID, COUNT(*) AS duplicate_count
FROM ATFM.T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON
WHERE FLIGHTDATE >= DATE '2026-07-01'
  AND FLIGHTDATE <  DATE '2026-08-01'
GROUP BY ID
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

SELECT FLIGHT_ID, COUNT(*) AS duplicate_count
FROM ATFM.T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON
WHERE FLIGHTDATE >= DATE '2026-07-01'
  AND FLIGHTDATE <  DATE '2026-08-01'
GROUP BY FLIGHT_ID
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

Khong duoc co dong nao. Neu ID khong phai khoa duy nhat, dung khoa P/U da

11.4. Doi chieu mau du lieu:

SELECT s.ID,
       s.FLIGHT_ID,
       s.FLIGHT_PK,
       s.PERM_ID,
       s.FLIGHTDATE,
       s.FLIGHTNBR,
       s.FROM_AIRP,
       s.TO_AIRP,
       s.ETD,
       s.ETA,
       s.ISACCESS
FROM ATFM_STAGE.T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON s
WHERE ROWNUM <= 20
ORDER BY s.FLIGHTDATE, s.ID;

Chon mot so ID tren va truy van lai ATFM.T_DAY_FLIGHTS_GOINGON, bao dam
FLIGHTDATE/FLIGHTNBR/FROM_AIRP/TO_AIRP/ETD/ETA/ISACCESS khop.

11.5. Kiem tra object invalid sau import:

SELECT object_name, object_type, status
FROM dba_objects
WHERE owner = 'ATFM'
  AND status <> 'VALID'
AND object_name LIKE '%DAY%FLIGHT%'
ORDER BY object_type, object_name;


BUOC 12 - DUNG KHAN CAP, ROLLBACK VA DON DEP
---------------------------------------------

Dung ngay quy trinh neu:

- Checksum khong khop hoac export/import log co ORA nghiem trong.
- Stage count khong bang source count, co row ngoai pham vi hoac ID/FLIGHT_ID trung.
- TARGET_JULY_COUNT khac 0 ma chua co phuong an merge duoc phe duyet.
- Import APPEND loi, missing_after_import khac 0 hoac phat sinh duplicate.
- Trigger/constraint bi invalid hoac ung dung hien thi sai lich.

Khong tu dong xoa du lieu de rollback khi co job khac da ghi vao bang sau
import. Bao toan dump/log va phoi hop DBA xac dinh tap ID can xu ly. Chi khi

       SUM(CASE WHEN FLIGHTDATE IS NULL THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS null_date_count,
